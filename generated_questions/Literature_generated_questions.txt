2. What is the significance of the epic genre in the context of The Odyssey, and how does it reflect the values and experiences of ancient Greek culture?  
- The Epic Genre is a long narrative poem representing values of culture and encompassing that culture’s experience, and The Odyssey is an epic that reflects the ideals and cultural standards of ancient Greek civilization.

3. How does the concept of "xenia" (hospitality) play a role in the narrative of The Odyssey, and what does it reveal about the social norms and values of ancient Greek society?  
- Xenia, the sacred rule of hospitality and reciprocity, is a key concept in The Odyssey, revealing the importance of social norms and values such as trust, respect, and the establishment of guest friendship in ancient Greek society.

4. What does the term "oikos" represent in the context of ancient Greek households, and how does it relate to the concept of a family unit and its role in society?  
- Oikos represents the household, encompassing the family, house, and property, and it is a fundamental unit of society where decisions are made and wealth is distributed, reflecting the importance of family and community in ancient Greek culture.

5. How does the character of Odysseus embody the themes of identity and recognition in The Odyssey, and what does his journey reveal about the nature of home and belonging?  
- Odysseus's journey reveals the fluidity of identity and the importance of recognition and acceptance within the household, showing how his return to Ithaca is a journey to reclaim his identity and restore order in his oikos.

6. In what ways does The Odyssey reflect the values of conservatism and the importance of maintaining traditional roles and order in society, particularly through the actions of Odysseus and the character of Penelope?  
- The Odyssey reflects the values of conservatism through Odysseus's actions, which represent a triumph of traditional values over forces trying to destroy the suitors, and through Penelope's fidelity, which is essential to preserving the honor and order of the household.
