1. Using the General Addition Rule, if the probability of event A is 0.4 and the probability of event B is 0.5, and the probability of both A and B occurring is 0.2, what is the probability of A or B occurring?  
   - P (A U B) = P(A) + P(B) - P (A and B)

2. If the probability of event A is 0.3 and the probability of event B is 0.4, and A and B are independent events, what is the probability of both A and B occurring?  
   - If events are independent, P(A and B) = P(A) x P(B)

3. Given that the probability of event A is 0.7, what is the probability of event A not occurring?
