GOAL
--------------------------------------------------

A completely new student should understand Calibrate in approximately 15–30 seconds.

The page should visually explain:

1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5

The user should understand:

“I answer a few questions,
Calibrate gives me a starting technique,
I add my real study material,
I review AI-generated questions and study,
then Calibrate uses my results to learn what works.”

This should feel like a short guided product tour, not a generic marketing features page.
